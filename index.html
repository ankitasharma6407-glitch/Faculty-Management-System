About FMPS

Faculty Management & Productivity System

A smart digital platform developed to simplify faculty administration, improve productivity and enhance academic management at Patna Women's College.

Mission

Digitize faculty operations with a secure, efficient and transparent management system.

Vision

Build a technology-driven academic environment that supports excellence and innovation.

Objectives

Reduce paperwork, automate daily tasks and improve faculty productivity.

Benefits

Faster workflow, centralized records and real-time academic monitoring.

Why Choose FMPS

Modern UI, secure access, role-based modules and easy management for every department.

Digital Workflow

Attendance, leave, reports, notices and performance tracking from one platform.

Patna Women's College Timeline
